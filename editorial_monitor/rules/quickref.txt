Syndication quick reference
The rules that matter most, on one page. For the full detail, open the guide for that topic.
The 10 golden rules
1. Sentence case for everything. Headlines, H2s, SEO titles. No title case, ever.
2. News only, and fresh. News stories must be under 24 hours old. Social stories (TikTok, Reddit, X) can be up to a week old.
3. The answer goes first. Tell the reader what happened right away.
4. Don’t just retell the video. The reader already watched it. Add something new. Only retelling the video breaks our rules and our partners’ rules.
5. Charged is not convicted. Until a court says guilty, it’s “allegedly.”
6. Religion, LGBTQ+ and medical topics are never the main topic. They can only come up in rare cases, with high-level approval.
7. The headline must match the video. If it didn’t happen on screen, it doesn’t go in the title.
8. Interlinks (two on all sites, one on Attack), one embed, one Imager image. Every article.
9. SEO title only. Leave every other SEO box as is.
10. Read your old notes. A note on one article is a note for all of them.
Before you submit
Headline
* Sentence case, under 150 characters
* Says only what the source shows, with anything unproven hedged
* No dashes, no colon joining two titles, single quotes, no bad words
* No words like rape or molestation or something like that in headings, must be ‘SA’
* Quotes only come from the source, not from the comments
Article
* Three paragraphs, then the H2, then a paragraph, then the embed
* Source named and linked in the first three paragraphs
* The answer is at the top (inverted pyramid)
* Adds something beyond the video, with no section of comments
* H2 is a sentence-case comment, no period
* Every paragraph is 2+ sentences and 5 lines or fewer
* 400+ words
* Neutral tone: no jokes, sarcasm or opinions
* Charged people are “accused” or “allegedly” the whole way through
* Quotes checked word for word, and only from the source, never from the comments
* Curse words censored (f*** or ****)
* Movie and show titles italicized, outlets not italicized
Links and embed
* Enough interlinks (two, or one on Attack), 3 to 5 words each, describing the story they link to
* No U-turns, no repeated links, no two links in one paragraph
* Embed is in place
Image
* From the Imager, 1280 x 720, matches the headline
* Title describes the picture; credit reads “Image by ___, license”
* Nothing on the MSN or NewsBreak banned list (no guns, no gore)
WordPress
* SEO title filled in, and nothing else in the SEO section
* Existing tags only, names not common nouns
* Primary category is News
You
* You checked your old revision notes
