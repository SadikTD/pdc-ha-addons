    How a switch-off is explained

    The breaker doesn't report why it switched off, so each switch-off is matched against the readings in the minute around it and against Home Assistant's activity log. If voltage, current, power or temperature was at a protection limit, that's the reason. If someone switched it in Home Assistant, you'll see who. Anything else was done in the Smart Life app or at the breaker. If the breaker ever reports a fault code, that replaces the guess.

      No SMS? Enter it by hand

      Press the meter's balance key (or check the display) and type the taka shown. From then on the balance is counted down from your use, and recharges are added on top. Check it now and then to keep it exact.
